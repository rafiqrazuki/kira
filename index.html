          Zakat

            The nisab follows the gold price and differs by state. The figure set here is Selangor's for July to December 2026, so check your own zakat office. Savings means bank, fixed deposit, ASB, unit trusts, shares, gold and crypto at their latest balance. Tabung Haji pays zakat for its depositors, and EPF is due only when you withdraw it.

            Income tax

              Resident rates for years of assessment 2023 to 2026. Individual relief (RM9,000) and EPF relief (up to RM4,000) are already applied. Put the rest under other reliefs: lifestyle up to RM2,500, life insurance or takaful up to RM3,000, spouse RM4,000, RM2,000 for each child under 18. This is an estimate; LHDN's e-Filing gives the final figure.
